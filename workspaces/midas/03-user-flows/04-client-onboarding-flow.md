# Client Onboarding Flow (Phase 2): Bringing On a New Client

**Who this is for**: You, the owner, now acting as an investment advisor who wants to bring on a new client.

**Prerequisites**: You have completed your RIA (Registered Investment Advisor) registration, have your compliance procedures in place, carry errors and omissions insurance, and have been running Midas successfully on your own money for at least 6 months.

**What happens at the end**: A new client's money is being managed by Midas under your advisory relationship.

---

## Step 1: The Initial Conversation (Before Midas is Involved)

### What happens

This starts offline. You meet a potential client -- maybe a friend, a referral, a business contact. They are interested in having you manage their investments. This conversation happens over coffee, on a phone call, or via email. Midas is not involved yet.

### What you need to discuss

Before using Midas at all, you need to cover:

- How much they want to invest (your minimum, if you have one)
- Their general financial situation (income, debts, other investments)
- What they expect in terms of returns and communication
- Your fees (what you will charge them)
- That their money will be managed by an automated system with your oversight
- That you are a registered investment advisor with fiduciary responsibility

### What you should have ready

Midas generates a "Client Introduction Packet" you can share:

> **Introduction Packet Contents:**
>
> - Your Form ADV Part 2A (disclosure brochure -- legally required before signing)
> - A plain-language explanation of how Midas works
> - Your fee schedule
> - Performance history (your own portfolio, with appropriate disclaimers)
> - What to expect as a client (communication frequency, reporting, how to reach you)

### Decision point (yours)

Do you want to take this person on as a client? Consider:

- Is the account size worth your time and their fees?
- Are they someone you can work with for years?
- Do they understand and accept automated management?

## Step 3: The Client's Onboarding Experience

### What the client sees and does (you are not involved -- this is self-service)

**Screen 1: Welcome**

> "Welcome, [Client Name]. [Your Name] will be managing your investments using a sophisticated automated system. Let's get you set up. This takes about 15-20 minutes."

**Screen 2: Identity Verification (KYC)**

> "We need to verify your identity. This is required by federal law for all investment accounts.
>
> Please provide:
>
> - Full legal name
> - Date of birth
> - Social Security Number (encrypted, never shared)
> - Current address
> - Phone number
> - A photo of your government-issued ID (driver's license or passport)
>
> [Upload ID Photo]"

Behind the scenes, Midas sends this information to a third-party verification service. The client sees a brief loading screen, then one of two results:

**If verified:**

> "Identity verified. Let's continue."

**If there is a problem:**

> "We need a little more information to verify your identity. This sometimes happens when there is a recent address change. Please contact [Your Name] for next steps."

**Screen 3: Risk Questionnaire**

The client answers 8-10 questions in plain language. These are similar to what any financial advisor would ask, but friendlier:

> **Question 1:** "In general, how do you feel about financial risk?"
>
> - I avoid risk -- protecting what I have is most important
> - I can tolerate some ups and downs for better long-term growth
> - I am comfortable with significant swings for the chance of higher returns
> - I am very aggressive -- I can handle big losses for big potential gains
>
> **Question 2:** "If your investments dropped 20% ($200,000 on a $1M portfolio) in a single month, what would you do?"
>
> - Sell everything immediately
> - Sell some to reduce my risk
> - Do nothing and wait for recovery
> - Buy more -- things are on sale
>
> **Question 3:** "When do you plan to start using this money?"
>
> - Within 1-3 years
> - In 3-10 years
> - In 10-20 years
> - 20+ years or never (this is generational wealth)
>
> **Question 4:** "What is this money relative to your total financial picture?"
>
> - This is most of my savings
> - This is about half of my savings
> - This is a smaller portion of my total wealth
> - I have significant other assets and income
>
> **Question 5:** "What is your primary goal for these investments?"
>
> - Preserve what I have (keep pace with inflation)
> - Moderate growth with limited risk
> - Strong growth over time (I accept short-term losses)
> - Maximum growth (I have a long time horizon and high risk tolerance)

... (and 3-5 more questions about income, major upcoming expenses, etc.)

**Screen 4: Results and Recommended Portfolio**

Based on their answers, Midas calculates a risk score and maps it to a suitable portfolio:

> "Based on your responses, here is your risk profile:
>
> **Risk Tolerance: Moderate Growth (Score: 6 out of 10)**
>
> This means you are comfortable with some market ups and downs in exchange for better long-term growth. You do not need this money in the near term.
>
> **Your Recommended Portfolio:**
>
> | Category            | Allocation | What this means                      |
> | ------------------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------ |
> | US Stocks           | 45%        | Broad exposure to American companies |
> | International       | 15%        | Companies outside the US             |
> | Bonds               | 30%        | Stability and income                 |
> | Real Estate (REITs) | 5%         | Real estate investment trusts        |
> | Cash                | 5%         | Available for needs                  |
>
> This portfolio is designed to grow steadily while limiting the worst-case scenarios. Over the last 20 years, a portfolio like this would have averaged 6-8% annual returns, with a worst year of about -18%.
>
> [This looks right for me] [I'd like to discuss with my advisor]"

**Screen 5: Advisory Agreement**

> "Please review and sign your Investment Advisory Agreement.
>
> Key terms:
>
> - [Your Name] will manage your investments on a discretionary basis (meaning trades are placed without asking you each time)
> - Annual advisory fee: [X]% of your portfolio value (deducted quarterly)
> - You can withdraw your money or end the agreement at any time
> - [Your Name] has a fiduciary duty to act in your best interest
>
> Full agreement: [Scrollable document]
>
> By signing, you agree to the terms above and confirm that you have received the Form ADV Part 2A disclosure brochure.
>
> [Sign electronically]"

**Screen 6: Brokerage Account**

> "Your investments will be held at Interactive Brokers, one of the largest and most secure brokerages in the world. Your money is held in YOUR name at IBKR -- [Your Name] can manage it but cannot withdraw it.
>
> [I already have an IBKR account -- Connect it]
> [I need to open a new IBKR account -- Start application]"

If opening a new account, the client is guided through IBKR's account application (which Midas pre-fills with the information already collected). This step can take a few business days for IBKR to approve.

**Screen 7: Confirmation**

> "You are all set. Here is a summary:
>
> - Identity: Verified
> - Risk profile: Moderate Growth (6/10)
> - Portfolio strategy: Moderate Growth
> - Advisory fee: [X]% per year
> - Agreement: Signed
> - Brokerage: [Connected / Application pending]
>
> What happens next:
> [Your Name] will review your information and approve your account. Once approved, your money will be invested according to your recommended portfolio over the next 5-10 trading days.
>
> You will receive a welcome email with login details for your client dashboard, where you can see your portfolio at any time.
>
> Questions? Contact [Your Name] at [contact info]."

## Step 4: You Review and Approve the Client

### What you see

In your Midas dashboard, under "Clients," you see a new pending entry:

> **New Client Pending Approval**
>
> Name: [Client Name]
> Risk score: 6/10 (Moderate Growth)
> Recommended portfolio: Moderate Growth
> Account size: $500,000
> Account type: Individual
> Fee: [X]% per year (estimated annual revenue: $[X])
> KYC status: Verified
> Agreement: Signed
> Brokerage: Connected (account #[partially masked])
>
> **Risk questionnaire answers:** [View details]
> **Suitability check:** Portfolio matches risk score. No concerns flagged.
>
> [Approve and begin investing] [Contact client first] [Decline]

### Decision points

- Does the recommended portfolio match what you discussed with the client?
- Is the risk profile consistent (no contradictory answers in the questionnaire)?
- Are you comfortable with this client and account size?
- Do you want to override the recommended portfolio? (Midas lets you adjust, but requires documenting why)

### If you approve

> "Client [Name] approved. Midas will begin investing their $500,000 according to the Moderate Growth portfolio over the next 5-10 trading days.
>
> The client will receive:
>
> - A welcome email today
> - A notification when the first trades are placed
> - Their first weekly summary next Sunday"

## Step 5: The Client's Money Gets Invested

### What the client sees (automated, you do not need to do anything)

**Day 1 email:**

> "Hi [Client Name],
>
> Your account has been approved. [Your Name] has begun investing your portfolio.
>
> Today's activity:
>
> - Invested $100,000 in US Stock Market fund
> - Invested $75,000 in Bond fund
> - Remaining $325,000 will be invested over the next 4-7 trading days
>
> You can log in to your client dashboard anytime to see your portfolio: [Login Link]"

**Over the next week**, the client receives brief daily updates showing progress until the portfolio is fully invested.

### What you see

On your owner dashboard, the client appears in your client list with a status indicator:

> [Client Name] | $500,000 | Deploying (Day 3 of 7) | Moderate Growth | +0.2% since start

## Timeline Summary

| ---- | ------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------- |
| 1    | You + Client | Initial conversation and agreement to proceed    | 1-2 meetings  |
| 2    | You          | Send onboarding invitation from Midas            | 2 minutes     |
| 3    | Client       | Complete KYC, risk questionnaire, sign agreement | 15-20 minutes |
